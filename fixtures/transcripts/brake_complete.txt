Verified customer concern while performing test drive. Found harsh squeal from the front axle when braking under 30 mph. Found front brake pads worn down to 2mm with the wear indicators contacting the rotors. Front rotors heavily grooved on both sides measured at 22.5mm which is below the 25mm minimum. Removed faulty worn brake pads and below-minimum rotors and installed new pads and rotors in reverse order. Torqued caliper bracket bolts to 110 Nm and wheel lug nuts to 108 Nm. Test drove vehicle for 5 miles and inspected. Found no more squeal when braking and braking feels smooth. All operation normal as factory design.
