Pipeline

Voice and bay video in. A fileable 3Cs story out — only if the measurements exist.

01

Capture

Device SHA-256, then a presigned S3 PUT. Prefix is rooftop / RO / hash. Same bytes never enqueue twice.

02

Transcribe · extract

Workers claim jobs with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED. Transcript and story versions are immutable rows.

03

Validate

OEM rules, not the LLM. Missing pad, rotor, torque, DTC, or road-test miles → needs_followup.

04

DMS writeback

Idempotency-Key, retries on 503, dead-letter after five. Ops can break the next write on purpose.

What to show

Four beats in two minutes.

Brake · complete

Stages flip, a 3Cs story files, lineage shows s3://, SHA-256, prompt_version, validator_version.

Missing measurements

Status is needs_followup. The claim does not file. Fill pad / rotor / torque / miles and it files as story v2.

Replay + DLQ

Same hash is a no-op. Inject one DMS 503 to retry; inject five to dead-letter. Dummy MP4/MPS sit in the object store.
